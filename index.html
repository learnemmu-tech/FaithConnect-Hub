The Problem We Solve

Why Churches Need FaithConnectHub

Most churches manage their community across disconnected tools — WhatsApp, YouTube, spreadsheets, and payment apps. Important information gets lost. Members feel disconnected. FaithConnectHub gives your church one organized, permanent digital home.

📱

Not Just Another Chat App

WhatsApp is great for quick messages. But sermons get buried, prayer requests disappear, and new members don't know where to join. FaithConnectHub is your church's structured digital home — not a group chat.

📂

Content That Lasts Forever

A sermon published today stays organized and searchable months later. Members can find teaching by topic, Bible verse, speaker, or date — not by scrolling through years of chat history.

👥

Structured Member Management

Every member joins through an approved link. You see who is in your church, approve requests, manage roles, and build a real member directory — not just a WhatsApp contact list.

💰

Organized Church Giving

Create named donation campaigns — Building Fund, Mission Fund, General Offering. Track contributions, see reports, and give your congregation a professional place to give.

🏢

Multi-Branch Ready

Managing churches in Hyderabad, Chennai, and Bangalore? One organization account. Each church has its own admin, members, and content. Everything separate. Everything visible from one dashboard.

🌍

Any Language, Any Region

Publish sermons, songs, and articles in Telugu, English, Hindi, Tamil, Spanish — any language your congregation speaks. FaithConnectHub is not just for English-speaking churches.

Community & Content

Built Around Your Church Community

Every feature is designed around how real churches operate — not adapted from generic business software.

🙏

Prayer Wall

Members submit prayer requests that go to the admin for review and approval. Once approved, the prayer is visible to the entire church community. Members respond with "I Prayed" and the prayer count increases in real time. Prayer becomes a shared community experience — not a message buried in a WhatsApp chat.

  • Submit prayer requests with category and description
  • Admin approves, rejects, or hides requests
  • Community "I Prayed" with real-time count
  • Anonymous prayer option available
📖

Sermon Library

Every sermon published stays organized, categorized, and searchable. Members find teaching from months or years ago by topic, Bible reference, speaker, or date. Sermons include video links, audio files, Bible references, and downloadable attachments — never lost in scroll history.

  • Video and audio sermon support
  • Bible reference tagging
  • Categories, tags, and series grouping
  • Members can bookmark and share
🎵

Worship Song Library

A complete, searchable library of worship songs with full lyrics in any language — Telugu, English, Hindi, Tamil, Spanish, or any other language your congregation uses. Worship teams and members access lyrics instantly. Songs can be bookmarked and favorited for quick access during service.

  • Multi-language lyrics support
  • Search by title, artist, or lyrics
  • Bookmark and favorite songs
  • Chord support available
📅

Events & Gatherings

Churches publish upcoming activities — Sunday services, Bible studies, youth meetings, conferences, prayer meetings, and community events. Members have one place to see everything happening in their church. Events include RSVP tracking so admins know who is coming.

  • Online and offline event support
  • RSVP and attendance tracking
  • Event calendar view for members
  • Notification sent when event published
💰

Donation Campaigns

Create named donation campaigns so your congregation knows exactly where their giving goes. Building Fund, Mission Fund, Youth Ministry, General Offering — each campaign has a goal, description, and real-time tracking. Admins see donation history, reports, and can export records.

  • Multiple campaigns simultaneously
  • Goal tracking with real-time progress
  • Donor history per campaign
  • Export donation reports
📝

Articles & Announcements

Publish Bible teachings, devotionals, church announcements, and study materials using a rich text editor. Articles are categorized, tagged, and searchable. Members can read, bookmark, and share content. Draft articles before publishing to review them first.

  • Rich text editor with formatting
  • Draft before publishing
  • Categories and tags
  • Members can bookmark and share

User Roles

The Right Access for Every Person

FaithConnectHub has three distinct user roles. Each person sees exactly what they need — nothing more, nothing less.

🏛️

Organization Admin

For multi-site organizations only

  • Create and manage multiple churches
  • Invite and assign Church Admins by email
  • View organization-wide analytics
  • Switch between churches from one account
  • Manage organization settings and billing
  • Full visibility across all churches
⛪

Church Admin

Manages one assigned church

  • Approve or reject member requests
  • Publish sermons, articles, and songs
  • Create and manage events
  • Create donation campaigns
  • Approve and manage prayer requests
  • View church analytics and member list
  • Manage church settings and join link
🙋

Church Member

Access to their approved church

  • Read and watch sermons and articles
  • Browse and bookmark worship songs
  • View and RSVP for upcoming events
  • Submit and view prayer requests
  • Click "I Prayed" on community prayers
  • View and contribute to donation campaigns
  • Receive church notifications
